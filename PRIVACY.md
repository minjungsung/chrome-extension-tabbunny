# TabBunny privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-21.

## Data and access

Local collections contain names, page URLs and titles, pinned flags, and creation timestamps.

`tabs` reads the current window's URLs, titles, and pinned state when you use the app. `storage` saves your burrows on this device. No website content is read.

Processing is local. The developer receives no extension data. No accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics or remote executable code. Google and Chrome have separate privacy policies.

## Optional feedback

Successful-use counts, activity dates, and a dismissal/opened preference are stored locally for an optional one-time review notice after at least five uses on three days spanning one week. No feedback telemetry is transmitted. The help page always links to the store review page, regardless of usage or satisfaction.

## Control and retention

Saved data remains until deleted, extension storage is cleared, or the extension is uninstalled. Exported files and clipboard contents remain under your control and may contain private content. Use the library/collection delete and backup controls.

## Contact

Use this repository’s issue tracker or the store support contact. Never post private information publicly.
