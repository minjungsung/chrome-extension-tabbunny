# TabBunny launch material

## Community post

Title: Save this window. Resume it later.

I built TabBunny, a Chrome extension for a simpler everyday browsing workflow.

TabBunny saves the web tabs in your current Chrome window so you can reopen their links later. Finish today’s research, study, or work and pick it up in a new window when you are ready.

What it does:
- One click to save your web tabs
- Reopen saved links in a new window
- Optional projects and JSON backups

Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gmggibbkhagclmcdlcgimijalmedhmhj

I am the developer. I would appreciate feedback on the workflow, confusing steps, or missing essentials. Please share your own experience; there is no reward or required rating.
