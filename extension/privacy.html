Privacy and help

Stored on your device

This extension does not send your data to a developer server. It contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote executable code.

Saved content uses Chrome local storage. Clearing extension data or uninstalling removes it. Chrome and its Web Store operate under Google's separate policies.

How this product accesses data

Local collections contain names, page URLs and titles, pinned flags, and creation timestamps.

Control your data

Use the export and delete controls where provided. Exported files may contain private URLs or notes, so store them somewhere you trust.

Support

Use the support contact on this extension's Chrome Web Store listing to report a bug. Include the extension version and steps to reproduce. Do not include passwords, private URLs, or sensitive notes.

Remove the extension

Open chrome://extensions, find this extension, and choose Remove.
